# 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from the **University of Cyprus** and a full stack developer with a particular interest in **e-commerce, business software, and automation**.

A big part of my experience comes from working alongside a family business that has been operating in the wholesale trade since **1957**. That gave me the opportunity to see software from both sides: not just as something you build, but as something people actually depend on every day.

I've built and maintained e-commerce stores, internal management tools, APIs, automation workflows, and mobile applications. A lot of my work starts with a simple question:

> **"Why are we doing this manually?"**

If something can be made simpler with software, I usually end up building it.

I work mainly with **JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Node.js, PHP, Python, WordPress, WooCommerce, MySQL, and React Native**, while also experimenting with new technologies when a project calls for them.

# 🧠 How I Like to Work

I enjoy working on projects where software has a **real purpose**.

That might be an online store, a warehouse system, an internal dashboard, an automation that removes repetitive work, or a mobile application built around a specific problem.

I'm interested in the entire process:

**idea → design → development → deployment → maintenance**

I also like understanding the business problem before writing the code. Sometimes the right solution isn't another feature. Sometimes it's a small automation, a better workflow, or removing a step altogether.

# ⭐ Projects

## 🔐 Sentinel

A mobile security project focused on keeping passwords and sensitive documents securely stored.

Built with **React Native**, with encrypted local data, biometric authentication, and secure synchronization.

The project also explores checking stored credentials against known data breaches and building a practical security-focused mobile experience.

**Tech:** `React Native` `Firebase` `CryptoJS`

---

## 🛒 B2B Catalog & Ordering Platform

A concept for a lightweight B2B ordering platform designed around the way wholesale businesses actually work.

The idea includes customer-specific catalog links, controlled access, customer-specific pricing, quote-only products, order history, repeat orders, product notes, and a simplified ordering interface.

The goal is to make B2B ordering easier without forcing a traditional consumer e-commerce workflow onto wholesale customers.

**Tech:** `React` `Node.js` `Express` `MySQL`
